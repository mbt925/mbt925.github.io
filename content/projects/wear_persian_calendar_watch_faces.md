Persian Calendar Watch Faces is a collection of watch faces for Wear OS inspired by Persian art and architecture, from the reliefs of Persepolis to the tilework of Isfahan. Each face shows the current Persian date and comes with three color themes and several date styles, from just the day to the full date.

The faces are built with Watch Face Format, so they are lightweight and battery friendly. They work on Wear OS 5 and newer.

---

### Privacy Policy
Persian Calendar Watch Faces don't collect any user data. All data are hosted locally on the device.
