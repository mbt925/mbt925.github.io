Game Watch Faces is a collection of retro pixel-art watch faces for Wear OS, each one inspired by a classic arcade game, from Pong and Pac-Man to Tetris. The game plays itself on your wrist while the clock is part of the action: the time is the score, the stack of blocks or the shields of the invaders. Date, battery and a score are always at hand, and the ambient mode keeps the time, date and battery.

The faces are built with Watch Face Format, so they are lightweight and battery friendly. They work on Wear OS 5 and newer.

---

### Privacy Policy
Game Watch Faces don't collect any user data. All data are hosted locally on the device.
